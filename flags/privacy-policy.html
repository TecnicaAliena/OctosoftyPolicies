Privacy Policy for Flags

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Flags is an educational game for children. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, how data is stored, and how some external services may be used.

Information We Collect

Flags does not require account registration and does not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location. If you choose to use the world leaderboard, Google Play Games Services processes your Play Games gamer identity (gamertag, avatar and a pseudonymous player ID) and game score to provide that feature. If your Play Games profile is public, your gamertag, avatar, score and leaderboard position may be visible to other players in the world leaderboard.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores some data locally on the device to provide its features, including:

  • audio preferences such as music and sound effect settings
  • local leaderboard scores: the score of each completed game, the Play Games gamer name and avatar when the player is signed in, the time the game was played and the pseudonymous Play Games player ID used to keep scores from different profiles separate

This local leaderboard includes the games of every Play Games profile used on the device, so that players sharing a device can see each other's results. It is stored only on the device and is never sent to our servers. The player ID is used only on the device to associate a saved score with the correct Play Games profile. There is no Flags account system and no Flags backend. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes all of this local data.

Internet Access

The core gameplay works offline. Country data, shapes, globe assets, audio, and flag images used by the game are packaged locally with the app, and you can play without signing in to Google Play Games. Internet access is used only for the optional Google Play Games world leaderboard, which needs an internet connection and a Play Games sign-in.

The app may open an external PayPal page only if the user chooses to make a voluntary donation.

We do not operate our own analytics, advertising, or leaderboard backend for this app.

Google Play Games Services

The optional world leaderboard is provided by Google Play Games Services. Signing in is never required to play, and the player chooses to sign in from the world leaderboard screen. Once signed in, the app submits only the final score and the selected leaderboard category. When the player opens or returns to the world leaderboard, Flags may submit the best score already stored locally for that same Play Games profile. This allows a player who later changes the profile from private to public to publish the best result already earned on the device without replaying it. Google Play Games ignores a submitted score when it is worse than the score already stored online.

Google associates the submitted score with the player's Play Games profile and displays the gamertag, avatar, score and position according to the player's privacy and profile-visibility settings. Only players who share their Play Games game activity publicly appear in the public world leaderboard. A private profile can remain signed in and keep its local scores without appearing publicly. Flags can open the official Play Games profile page, but only the player can choose to make the profile public; Flags cannot read or change that setting.

The Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar are kept locally only to associate and label local games and synchronize that profile's best score. They are never sent to a Flags server. Scores and Play Games profile information used by the world leaderboard are sent directly to Google Play Games Services. Google manages this data under its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Flags is designed for children, but it is intended to be used with parental supervision when appropriate. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children. A parent or guardian should decide whether a child may use Google Play Games and participate in a public leaderboard.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with third-party services when needed for functionality, such as:

  • Google Play Games Services for optional sign-in and world leaderboards
  • PayPal for voluntary donations

These services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user personal data because the app does not collect such personal data through its own backend.

Data Security

Since the app mainly stores gameplay settings and scores locally on the device, data protection depends in part on the security of the device itself.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future if app features change. The updated version will be published at the same page address.
