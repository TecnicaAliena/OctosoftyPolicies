Privacy Policy for Flags

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Flags is an educational game for children. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect, how data is stored, and how some external services may be used.

Information We Collect

Flags does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as email address, phone number, or precise location.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores some data locally on the device to provide its features, including:

  • player name chosen in settings
  • audio preferences such as music and sound effect settings
  • local Top 10 leaderboard scores

This information is stored only on the device and is not sent to our servers.

Internet Access

The core gameplay works offline. Country data, shapes, globe assets, audio, and flag images used by the game are packaged locally with the app.

The app may open an external PayPal page only if the user chooses to make a voluntary donation.

We do not operate our own analytics or advertising backend for this app at this stage.

Children's Privacy

Flags is designed for children, but it is intended to be used with parental supervision when appropriate. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with third-party services when needed for functionality, such as:

  • public image hosting for some flag assets
  • PayPal for voluntary donations

These services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user personal data because the app does not collect such personal data through its own backend.

Data Security

Since the app mainly stores gameplay settings and scores locally on the device, data protection depends in part on the security of the device itself.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future if app features change. The updated version will be published at the same page address.

Contact

If you need to contact the developer regarding this Privacy Policy, you can use the contact information provided on the app listing or project page.
