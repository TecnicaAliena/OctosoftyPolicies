Educational geography game

Flags

Flags is a quiz game for learning the flags and country shapes of the world. Choose a difficulty, recognise countries, improve your score and challenge yourself in two complementary game modes.

  • Flag to country shape
  • Country shape to flag
  • Worldwide leaderboards

Google Play Games

Players can use their Google Play Games profile to publish scores to global leaderboards and display their Play Games gamer name and avatar. Flags does not operate its own account system or leaderboard server.
