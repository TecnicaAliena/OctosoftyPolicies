QuickPal Data Deletion

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Local Data And Accounts

QuickPal by Octosofty is free and has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics, or in-app purchases. Projects, package and pallet data, report settings, logos, and generated PDFs are processed locally. We do not operate a server that stores your projects.

Delete Data On Your Device

  • Delete exported project files using your Android file manager.
  • Delete reports from Documents/QuickPal and any other folders where you saved copies.
  • Clear QuickPal storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove private app data.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not necessarily delete exported documents, original logo images, backups, or files shared with other apps. Delete those copies separately. Use your device or backup provider's settings to manage Android backups.

Optional PayPal Contributions

A five-second support prompt appears before PDF exports. Payment is optional and unlocks no features. PayPal opens outside QuickPal only when you select a contribution. QuickPal does not receive payment credentials. PayPal and the recipient may hold transaction details, such as payer name, email, and amount. For data held by PayPal, use PayPal's privacy controls. For transaction data held by the developer, contact the address below. Payment records may need to be retained for applicable legal or accounting obligations; we will explain any applicable retention requirement when responding to your request.

Retention And Shared Files

Local project files and reports remain until you delete them. Third-party services control retention of files you choose to share or back up. QuickPal cannot remotely delete those copies.
