QuickPal Data Deletion

Last updated: June 4, 2026

How QuickPal Stores Data

QuickPal stores palletization projects, package data, pallet data, report settings, and generated files locally on your device. QuickPal does not operate a server account system for storing your projects.

How To Delete Local Data

You can delete QuickPal data directly on your device by:

  • Deleting saved project files that you created or exported.
  • Deleting generated PDF reports from the device Documents/QuickPal folder.
  • Using Android system settings to clear QuickPal app storage or uninstall the app.

Request Assistance

If you need help identifying or deleting QuickPal data, contact us at tecnicaaliena@gmail.com. Please include the app name, QuickPal, and describe the data you want help deleting.

Data Retention

Locally stored QuickPal project files and PDF reports remain on your device until you delete them. If you shared files with another app or service, deletion and retention by that third-party service is controlled by that service.

Data That May Be Handled By Third Parties

QuickPal may use Google Play services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. Requests related to data collected by Google services are handled according to Google's privacy and data controls.
